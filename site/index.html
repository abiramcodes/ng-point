One Alt+Click.
Your agent is already on it.

ng-point captures the element's component, file:line, and cleaned HTML, the exact context your agent needs, at a fraction of the tokens of a pasted screenshot. 

npm install -D ng-point

Up to 5 elements in one session, copied together with one Enter. Angular 21–22.

In every block
Component, file:line, template, cleaned HTML, directives, parent, and your typed instruction.
Shortcuts
Alt+Click picks, Alt+Shift+Click adds, Alt+↑/↓ retargets, Enter copies and queues, Esc discards.
Safe by default
Dev builds only, secrets redacted at capture, and ngPointIgnore opts any subtree out.
Works with
Angular 21–22 (the template line needs 22.1.4+), lazy routes, dialogs, and module-federation remotes.

MIT licensed. Dev-mode only by design.
